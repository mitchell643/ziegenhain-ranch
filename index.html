Our Story

A name from the old country

Ziegenhain is a town in Germany where the Knauf family once made their home. Generations later, that name has found a new place on a ranch outside Hico, Texas.

The Knaufs were Bavarian swordsmiths, and the family name itself means the pommel of a sword. That history is worked into the ranch crest: a Z for Ziegenhain, a sword driven through it, and a K set in the pommel for Knauf.

The ranch was established in 2026, and the work of building it is just getting started.
